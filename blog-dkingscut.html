Building a Booking First Website for D King's Cut in Strasbourg

Client: D King's Cut Live at: dkingscut.com Scope: Web development, booking flow, local SEO
Live

D King's Cut is a home visit barbering service in Strasbourg. There is no shop and no waiting room, so the website has one job: let a customer see what is on offer, pick a time, and book. Easywise Studio was brought on to design and build the site from the ground up and to set it up so local customers can find it.

The Build

The site is built with Next.js and designed mobile first, because most people booking a barber will do it from their phone. The look is dark and sharp, with a near black theme, a bold hero line ("Your cut, at your door."), and a single clear button to book.

  • A hero that explains the offer in one sentence and sends the visitor straight to the booking page
  • A services list with the duration and price of every option shown up front, from a 12 euro men's cut to the 50 euro VIP full service with hair treatment, so there are no surprises at the door
  • Add on services for black hair dye and full hair coloring, plus dedicated options for women's afro cuts and children's cuts
  • A gallery of recent work with more than 35 photos, so new customers can judge the quality before they book
  • Clear opening hours at the bottom of the page, with the booking button repeated so the visitor never has to scroll back up
  • A shop page, ready for products alongside the cuts
  • Mobile web app settings, including a theme color and a home screen title, so the site feels like an app when saved to a phone

The Booking Flow

For a one person service, missing a booking costs real money. The booking page lets a customer choose a service and a time slot, and every confirmed booking triggers an automatic notification email to the owner through Resend. The sending domain was verified with DKIM and SPF records so these emails land in the inbox and not in spam.

The setup is deliberately simple. Emails only go out when a booking is made, and there is no mailbox to maintain on the domain.

The SEO Approach

A home visit barber does not need to rank for the whole world. The goal is to be found by people in and around Strasbourg who are searching for a barber who comes to them.

Technical SEO. A meta description written around the core search intent (home visit barbering in Strasbourg), a fast Next.js build with optimised images, and a mobile responsive layout.

Local relevance. The city name and the home visit angle sit in the title, the headline, and the page copy, so search engines understand both where the service operates and what makes it different.

Visual proof. A large gallery of real work gives visitors a reason to stay on the page and gives the site fresh, relevant imagery for image search.

Results

The site is live and taking bookings online. Traffic and ranking figures will be added here as they come in.
