Building the Website and Growing the Search Presence for Glory Moses Digital

Client: Glory Moses Digital Live at: glorymoses.co Scope: Web design, full SEO
Live and Performing Well

Glory Moses is a founder and entrepreneur based in France building Glory Moses Digital, a venture focused on digital skills, online education, and practical opportunities in the modern economy. Easywise Studio was brought on to design and build the brand's website from the ground up and to run a full SEO campaign to grow its visibility online.

The Build

The site was built as a clean, modern founder profile and venture hub, structured around clear sections that guide a visitor from who Glory Moses is to what she is building. The homepage introduces her as founder and links out to the flagship venture, Glory Moses Digital, which serves as the master brand for the education and digital skills offering.

  • A founder led narrative structure that positions the brand clearly for a first time visitor
  • A dedicated ventures section highlighting Glory Moses Digital and its offerings, including practical digital skills, online courses, resources and tools, community support, and career growth
  • Multiple low friction contact paths, including email, WhatsApp, and a contact form, alongside links to X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and LinkedIn
  • An FAQ section that answers common visitor questions directly, which also supports SEO by matching real search queries people type in

The SEO Strategy

The campaign followed the same full scope approach used across other Easywise projects, covering three layers of work.

Technical SEO. Clean site structure, proper meta descriptions and Open Graph tags for social sharing, mobile responsive design, and fast load times to support both search rankings and the visitor experience.

Content strategy. On page content built around the founder's story and venture positioning, with FAQ style content designed to capture question based searches related to Glory Moses and Glory Moses Digital.

Backlinks and off site visibility. Brand presence extended across social platforms to build authority signals and drive referral traffic back to the site, alongside broader backlink building to strengthen domain authority.

Results

The site is live, fully functional, and performing well. The client has expressed satisfaction with both the build and the growth in visibility since launch. As real traffic and ranking figures come in, they will be added here.

Why It Matters as Proof of Work

This project shows the full range of what Easywise Studio offers, not just building a website, but building it with SEO and discoverability in mind from day one. It demonstrates the ability to translate a founder's personal brand and business vision into a site structure that works for both visitors and search engines.
