Overview

A Founder Brand, Built to Be Found

Glory Moses is a founder and entrepreneur based in France building Glory Moses Digital, a venture focused on digital skills, online education, and practical opportunities in the modern economy.

Easywise Studio was brought on to design and build the brand's website from the ground up, and to run a full SEO campaign to grow its visibility online. The brief called for both: a site that represents the founder clearly, and a strategy that gets it seen.

The Build

Clean, Founder-Led, Structured to Convert

The site was built as a clean, modern founder profile and venture hub, structured around clear sections: About, Ventures, Values, FAQ, and Contact. The homepage introduces Glory Moses as founder and links out to the flagship venture, Glory Moses Digital, which serves as the master brand for the education and digital skills offering.

  • Founder-led narrative — a Vision, Creation, Impact structure that positions the brand clearly for first-time visitors.
  • Dedicated Ventures section — highlighting Glory Moses Digital and its offerings: practical digital skills, online courses, resources and tools, community support, career growth.
  • Low-friction contact paths — email, WhatsApp, and a contact form, alongside links to X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and LinkedIn.
  • An FAQ section — answering the questions visitors actually ask (who Glory Moses is, what the venture does, where she's based), which does double duty by matching real search queries.

The SEO Strategy

Three Layers, Working Together

The campaign followed the same full-scope approach used across other Easywise projects, covering technical foundations, content, and off-site authority.

01

Technical SEO

Clean site structure, proper meta descriptions and Open Graph tags for social sharing, mobile-responsive design, and fast load times to support both rankings and user experience.

02

Content Strategy

On-page content built around the founder's story and venture positioning, with FAQ-style content designed to capture question-based searches related to Glory Moses and Glory Moses Digital.

03

Backlinks & Off-Site Visibility

Brand presence extended across X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and LinkedIn to build authority signals and drive referral traffic, alongside broader backlink building to strengthen domain authority.

Results

Live, Fully Functional, Performing Well

The site is live, fully functional, and performing well. The client has expressed satisfaction with both the build and the growth in visibility since launch. The project stands as a strong proof of work for combined web design and SEO delivery, showing the ability to take a personal brand from concept to a polished, discoverable online presence.
